Privacy

Effective October 14, 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send us through bolo.ltd.

What we collect

When you use the form on this site, we collect:

  • your email address
  • which option you picked (waitlist, product updates, or message)
  • your message, if you write one

We don't use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

How we use it

We use your email only to send the BoloTie news you asked for and to reply to your messages. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it for advertising.

Who handles it

Two services process form submissions for us:

  • Google (Google Sheets) stores the submissions.
  • FormSubmit forwards each submission to our inbox.

Like any website, this site also loads fonts and code from Google Fonts and unpkg. They receive your IP address as part of those requests.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it for BoloTie.

Your choices

Email info@bolo.ltd at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop hearing from us.

Do Not Track

We don't track you across websites, so we don't change anything in response to Do Not Track signals.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
