What does it do?

It runs. And keeps on running

Spring-wound Bolex cameras are among the most reliable and beloved tools in film history. Yet they're inherently limited by the short duration of their spring mechanism, and the few existing motor drives are bulky, costly, and extremely rare.

Enter BoloTie: an ultra compact, computer-controlled motor that lets you run any Bolex for an unlimited duration with sound-sync accuracy. It gives new functionality to our favorite cameras, and can bring worn or broken cameras back to life.

Works on any model

It is also the only existing motor (to our knowledge) that achieves crystal sync accuracy on the universal 8:1 shaft. What does that mean? BoloTie works with all models of Bolex cameras, from the Rex 1 to the Rex 5 to the SBM.

Same as ever, only more

The Bolex has always allowed filmmakers to create images that no other camera can. BoloTie is meant to follow in this spirit, allowing the camera to do what it does best: frame-by-frame animations, superimpositions, time lapses and custom frame rates, all with total portability and simplicity of use.

And it has a few new tricks up the sleeve, to push filmmakers ever further.

Tested & calibrated

Very, very high precision

Crystal sync, roll after roll

BoloTie has been tested and calibrated against an ultra-precise GPS clock, so it isn't just crystal-sync accurate in the moment, but over full rolls. At crystal speeds up to 64 fps, the motor drifts less than a fraction of a frame over a 400 ft roll — if you could even shoot that long!

Exact exposure, every frame

Single-frame and animation modes have been calibrated by cross-referencing the driver signal, motor encoder and an optical sensor across the gate, so every exposure lands precisely in sync with the shutter's movement.
