Project overview

A digital motor drive for the Bolex H16

BOLOTIE replaces the spring-wound motor mechanism of the Bolex H16 with a digitally controlled external motor mounted on the side of the camera. Any Rex 1–Rex 5 or SB/SBM body becomes a fully motorised, crystal-sync camera: continuous running for the length of the roll, continuous speeds up to 64 fps, and operating modes no existing accessory combines in a single unit.
